A bright villa in a quiet, gated condominium.

Located in the gated Patã Village condominium, in Patã de Baixo, Albufeira, this elegant 3-bedroom terraced villa, built in 2010, stands out for its contemporary architecture, excellent sun exposure and central location — close to shops, schools, golf courses and the best beaches in the Algarve.

On the ground floor, a welcoming living room benefits from a large east-facing panoramic window and a retractable glass system opening onto a balcony. The kitchen is independent and fully equipped. From the kitchen, a second terrace — also glass-enclosed — is currently used as a dining room, with a barbecue and direct garden access.

On the first floor, three bedrooms with built-in wardrobes and two bathrooms — the main suite with a private bathroom and a large open balcony. In the basement, a spacious garage for two to three vehicles, with potential for storage, a workshop or a private gym.
